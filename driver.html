Reliable and easy to use, with everything you need to drive and earn when you want.

  1. Accept a ride request: The app will automatically find passengers for you
  2. Pick up your passenger: Use the in-app navigation to reach the pickup location
  3. Drive to the destination: Follow the directions and follow local traffic rules
  4. Repeat to earn more money.

Frequently asked questions from drivers

Can I drive with Bolt in my city?

If the city where you live is listed here, you can!

How do I start driving with Bolt?

Complete the registration form, submit the required documents, and verify your identity to get started.

What if I don’t have a car?

We can connect you with Bolt’s trusted partners, who can help you rent a vehicle at a competitive price.

What are the requirements for the smartphone?

It must run at least Android 9.0 or iOS 16. If you don’t have one, support can help you pick an affordable device.

When do I get my earnings?

Our payment cycle runs from Monday 00:00 to the following Sunday at 23:59, after which you’ll receive a balance statement. If you’re due a payout, you’ll receive it on a weekly basis — no need to wait until the end of the month.
