Why drive with Bolt

Bolt operates in over 50 countries and connects millions of riders with driver partners every day. As an independent driver partner, you choose when to drive and which ride requests to accept.

Set your own schedule

Go online and offline in the app whenever it suits you. Drive on your own terms, whether alongside your everyday work or in your free time.

Fast payouts

Get your earnings regularly, with weekly or bi-weekly payouts, instead of waiting until the end of the month.

Regular payouts help you stay in control of your finances and plan ahead.

Large rider network

Bolt connects driver partners with a large network of riders across the city. A larger rider base on the platform increases your opportunities for receiving ride requests.

Transparent earnings

How much can you earn driving

Your earnings = fares + optional bonuses + tips This simple breakdown explains how your earnings are calculated and how different components add up when you drive with Bolt.

Your earnings depend on when, where, and how much you choose to drive. You can drive regularly or just take a few rides in your free time. Earnings are influenced by demand in your area, your vehicle category and how many driver partners are active nearby.

Earnings vary by market, demand, and activity.

Fares

In many markets, driver partners earn up to 75% of the fare per ride, but this may vary by location.

Earnings are calculated per completed ride and paid according to the local schedule.

Rewards

At times, Bolt runs campaigns to increase rider demand in specific areas or during certain hours. These can include peak-time offers or activity-based campaigns.

Terms vary by market and are shown in the app.

Tips

Riders can choose to leave a tip after a ride. Tips are added to your fare share and paid directly to you. Any tips you receive are yours in full and are included in your total earnings.

Frequently asked questions for driver partners

Find answers to common questions about driver earnings, payments, and driving with Bolt.

Can I drive with Bolt in my city?

Bolt is available in hundreds of cities worldwide. Check our Cities pages to see if we’re available for you to start driving and generating earnings in your area.

How do I register to drive with Bolt?

Registering to earn with Bolt is simple. Just fill in your details above and submit the required documents. Once your registration is approved and you’ve completed the sign-up steps, you’ll be ready to start earning on your own schedule.

Do I need a car to drive with Bolt?

Yes, but if you don’t own one, don’t worry—there are other options available. Our local teams can often help you find partner fleets or rental companies that offer competitive rates on accepted vehicles. In some cities, long-term renters can even access financing deals through our partners.

Do I need a smartphone to drive with Bolt?

You’ll need a smartphone that runs at least Android 9.0 or iOS 14 to use the Bolt Driver app. If you’re looking for a new device, our team can advise you on budget-friendly Android options that work perfectly with the platform.

Is driving with Bolt a good side hustle?

The biggest advantage of the Bolt platform is that you generate earnings on your own terms—making it a great flexible option. There are no minimum hours; you can drive all week or just on evenings and weekends. You’re the one in control.

How do I get paid for driving with Bolt?

Our payment cycle runs from Monday (00:00) to the following Sunday (23:59), after which you’ll receive a balance statement. If you have generated earnings due, you’ll receive them on a weekly basis in many cities, so there's no need to wait until the end of the month.
