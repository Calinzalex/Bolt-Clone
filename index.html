Meet Bolt 7

Bolt 7 is the next-generation scooter, built together with city authorities and shaped by what riders struggle with day to day. It combines a fully integrated display with navigation, real-time parking guidance, stronger safety features, and built-in AI systems to help reduce pavement riding and make shared scooters safer for everyone.
